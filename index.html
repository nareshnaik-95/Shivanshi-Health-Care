About Us

The First MBBS & MD Clinic in Nadergul

Bringing quality healthcare closer to your doorstep — because your health deserves the best.

Shivanshi Health Care is a trusted general physician and day care clinic located at Nadergul Bus Stop, Hyderabad. Established in 2023, we are proud to be the first MBBS & MD clinic in the Nadergul area, providing expert medical consultation to individuals and families.

Our doctors — Dr. Srinu Naik and Dr. Nirosha — are highly qualified with MBBS and MD degrees, and are dedicated to delivering compassionate, patient-centered care. We believe that quality healthcare should be accessible, affordable, and close to home.
